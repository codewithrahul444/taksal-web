Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, comprehensive answers about Taksal Studio's features, privacy, and architecture.

2 Vector Graphic Design Studio

Yes. Taksal renders vector graphics at 300 DPI (dots per inch) ultra-high precision print resolution. This standard matches the technical requirements of commercial offset printing presses for business cards, merchandise, banners, and flyers without pixelation.

You can export designs in high-resolution PNG (with transparency support), JPEG (with custom compression), crisp vector SVG, and commercial PDF documents generated directly on your device via iText7.

Magic Layers is an intelligent computer vision tool built into Taksal. It takes a flat bitmap image (such as an uploaded logo or graphic badge) and separates it into distinct, editable vector layers that you can reorder, recolor, scale, or delete individually.

Taksal's Thermal Receipt Generator prepares POS slips in standard 58mm and 80mm thermal paper widths. Connect your thermal printer via Bluetooth or USB OTG, install the printer's Android Print Service driver, and tap 'Print Receipt'.

4 Security, Hardware Encryption & Privacy

Your business data is stored exclusively on your device in a private SQLite Room database. Taksal does not operate a central user cloud database. Neither Taksal nor any third party has access to your customer lists, sales figures, designs, or invoices.

Sensitive values (such as business profile credentials and Gemini AI API keys) are encrypted using 256-bit AES-GCM cryptography. The cryptographic master key is generated and stored inside the Android device's hardware Secure Enclave / Trusted Execution Environment (TEE), meaning the key cannot be extracted even from rooted devices or compiled APK inspection.

Because all data lives on your device, you can completely erase everything at any time by going to Android Settings > Apps > Taksal > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the application. This immediately and permanently purges all local databases and cryptographic keys.
