Our Mission & Story

The Philosophy Behind Taksal

How we set out to eliminate recurring software fees for independent creators, shopkeepers, and micro-enterprises worldwide.

What Does "Taksal" Mean?

The word Taksal (टकसाल) historically refers to a Mint — the sovereign institution where legal currency, precious coins, and stamps of authentic value are crafted with meticulous precision.

In the modern commercial arena, an entrepreneur's "currency" is their brand identity and verified paperwork: the crisp 300 DPI business card they hand a client, the distinctive logo on their shop signboard, the professional GST invoice that seals a deal, and the instant UPI barcode on their counter. Taksal was built to give every creator their own pocket mint.

The Problem We Solved

The Subscription Trap of Modern Software

A solo business owner today is told they need:

Design Subscription

$12 to $25 / month just to export high-resolution PNGs or access basic brand palettes without watermarks.

Invoicing SaaS

$15 to $35 / month to issue more than 5 invoices or print thermal receipts, holding client data hostage.

QR & Barcode Utilities

$5 to $10 / month for simple barcode generators riddled with spammy link redirections.

Our Contrarian Manifesto

We believe basic commercial infrastructure — designing an identity, issuing an itemized bill, tracking stock, and accepting payments — is a fundamental human economic right that should not be leased like a luxury utility. By building an ultra-optimized native Android client that runs entirely on the user's hardware, Taksal eliminates cloud infrastructure overhead and offers 100% of its features completely free forever.

Engineering Principles

Built on Native Sovereign Architecture

Hardware-Backed Security (Android KeyStore)

Your business credentials, tax identifiers, brand profiles, and Gemini AI API keys are protected on-device with 256-bit AES-GCM encryption keys held within the phone's hardware Secure Enclave.

100% Local Storage (Room SQLite)

Your customer lists, invoice totals, and inventory are stored locally in SQLite tables on your device. We do not maintain any centralized user database, ensuring zero cloud breaches or surveillance.

Jetpack Compose & Google Skia

Crafted with modern declarative Kotlin and Jetpack Compose Material 3. Hardware-accelerated 60 FPS vector canvas rendering eliminates lag, even with hundreds of canvas layers.

Ethical Monetization with UMP Consent

Rather than paywalling features, Taksal uses non-disruptive Google AdMob ads with strictly regulated 60-second cooldown timers and full Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) privacy consent controls.
