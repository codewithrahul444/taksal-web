Professional GST Tax Invoices

Create complete, legally sound tax invoices in seconds. Taksal automatically computes item totals, sub-totals, and applicable GST/tax percentages, embedding your company's GSTIN, branding, and terms.

  • Automated Tax Calculation: Choose standard GST rates (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) or define custom international sales tax rates.
  • Dynamic UPI QR Integration: Every generated invoice includes your UPI ID so customers can point their phone camera and pay immediately.
  • One-Tap Sharing: Share vector PDFs directly via WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail, or print wirelessly via Android Print Service.

Thermal POS Cash Slips (58mm / 80mm)

Perfect for retail counters, grocery stores, cafes, and pop-up stalls. Generate ultra-compact thermal billing slips formatted with transaction ID, item breakdown, tax total, and cashier signature lines. Print directly to Bluetooth thermal printers.

Quotation & Proposal Maker
